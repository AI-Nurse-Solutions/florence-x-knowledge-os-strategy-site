# NAIO Collaborator Checklist

**One-page readiness screen for anyone joining, funding, piloting, teaching, publishing, or collaborating inside the NAIO / NIN / Florence-X / EDENA ecosystem.**

> Build the nurse, not the dependency.
>
> Cloud AI gives leverage. Local AI gives resilience. EDENA gives conscience. NIN gives trust.

## 1. Mission Fit

- [ ] This work strengthens dignity, safety, judgment, and nurse empowerment.
- [ ] It serves real workflow burden, learning, community, or governance need.
- [ ] It can be explained to a tired bedside nurse without hype or jargon.

## 2. Safety Boundary

- [ ] No PHI, credentials, secrets, or private patient context in foundational work.
- [ ] No autonomous diagnosis, orders, treatment, patient communication, or compliance authority.
- [ ] Clinical, legal, financial, and regulatory claims are reviewed by accountable humans.

## 3. EDENA Gate

- **Green:** learning, reflection, no-PHI productivity.
- **Yellow:** decision support or team influence.
- **Orange:** operational workflows, dashboards, agents.
- **Red:** clinical, legal, finance, PHI, autonomy.

## 4. Stewardship Questions

- [ ] What good must scale?
- [ ] What must remain human-led?
- [ ] Who is the accountable owner?
- [ ] What data is allowed or forbidden?
- [ ] What could harm dignity, equity, or trust?
- [ ] What is the stop condition?
- [ ] What is the manual fallback?
- [ ] How will proof of good be measured?

## 5. Stop Signs

- [ ] Cannot name owner, tier, data boundary, or rollback path.
- [ ] Claims sound validated but proof is absent.
- [ ] Speed increases while accountability becomes less visible.
- [ ] Nurses are treated as users instead of stewards.

## 6. Ready to Collaborate When

- [ ] The work passes mission fit, safety boundary, and EDENA tiering.
- [ ] It builds transferable skill, local memory, trust, and fallback capacity.
- [ ] It preserves the doctrine: Agents propose. Humans judge. Nurses steward.

## 7. Collaborator Pledge

I will protect dignity before I optimize work. I will preserve human judgment before I pursue automation. I will separate claim from proof, speed from safety, and adoption from readiness.

## 8. Use This as the Pre-Meeting Screen

**Meeting rule:** if a proposed project cannot pass this one-page screen, it does not move to build, pitch, publish, pilot, or partner review. Tier up when ambiguous. Slow down when safety, dignity, PHI, clinical judgment, finance, legal, compliance, or public trust are touched.
