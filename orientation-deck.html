06 / 10
EDENA governance

Autonomy rises only when stewardship rises.

Green

Education, reflection, no-PHI personal productivity.

Yellow

Decision support, team influence, strategy, research.

Orange

Operational dashboards, agents, integrations, monitoring.

Red

Clinical, legal, financial, PHI-heavy, or autonomous authority.

When ambiguous, tier up. Scarcity is not permission to lower standards.
Every agent needs owner, purpose, boundary, gate, audit, stop condition.Red is blocked until explicitly governed.
